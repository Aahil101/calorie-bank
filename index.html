Passbook Architecture: How Your Caloric Journal Works
1. Opening Balance (0.00 kcal)

At your custom 24h cycle reset time, your ledger opens at 0.00 kcal. It represents the starting state before meals are eaten or passive basal calories are drawn.

2. Cycle Intake (Deposits)

Every meal, snack, or drink you log credits positive calories (+ kcal) into your bank, creating an energy surplus.

3. Workouts (Withdrawals)

Physical workouts and athletic training withdraw energy (− kcal) from your ledger based on standardized MET coefficients.

4. Passive Basal Burn

Your body expends energy continuously 24/7 to power your brain, heart, respiration, and cellular repair. This automatically draws down your balance second-by-second.

5. Net Cycle Balance

Net Balance = Deposits − Withdrawals − Passive Burn. Positive values indicate a caloric surplus; negative values indicate an active deficit.
